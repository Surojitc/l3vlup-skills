# Equity Research & Hedge Funds — L3VLUP Skills

The public-markets research loop — earnings, filings, pitches, catalysts.

| Skill | Job to be done | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Earnings Teardown](./earnings-teardown/SKILL.md) | Understand what actually changed this quarter — before the market finishes deciding. | One-page earnings delta memo + questions list | ~25 min |
| [Filing Diff](./filing-diff/SKILL.md) | Find what materially changed between this filing and the last one. | Material-changes memo with source citations | ~20 min |
| [Stock Pitch Builder](./stock-pitch-builder/SKILL.md) | Turn research into a pitch with a variant perception, not a book report. | Pitch architecture: thesis, variant view, valuation, catalysts, risks | ~45 min |
| [Catalyst Monitor](./catalyst-monitor/SKILL.md) | Track the events that will prove your thesis right or wrong — before they happen. | Catalyst calendar + monitoring plan with triggers | ~20 min |
